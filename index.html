Mission briefing

You are taking over one shift at a small lunar outpost.

Your job is to keep four connected systems working until handover. You will face seven situations. Each choice has a cost, so the goal is not to keep every number at 100 — it is to keep the whole base alive.

Life SupportProtect breathable air, water recovery and basic habitat support.
PowerManage generated and stored electrical energy.
FoodBalance stored supplies and small-scale crop production.
Radiation ProtectionLimit the crew's exposure during risky conditions.
Rule: If any system reaches 0, the shift fails. Finish all seven situations with every system above 0 to complete the mission.
Independent student prototype. No NASA logo, mission patch, flag or AI-generated image is used in this game. Scientific concepts are simplified for educational gameplay.

How it works

You manage four connected systems during one lunar-base shift. Every event gives you two possible actions. Neither option is perfect: saving one resource can put pressure on another.

  • Life Support represents breathable air, water recovery and basic habitat support.
  • Power represents stored and generated electrical energy.
  • Food represents stored food plus small-scale crop production.
  • Radiation Protection represents the crew's protection from space radiation exposure.

If any system reaches 0, the shift ends. Complete all seven events with every system above 0 to finish the mission.
